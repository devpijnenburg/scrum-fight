ScrumFight features

Everything your Scrum team needs to estimate user stories together, in refinement, sprint planning or wherever you discuss the backlog.

Estimating together

⚡ Real-time voting

Everyone picks a card at the same time. Others only see that you voted, not what, until the reveal.

🃏 Simultaneous reveal

All cards flip at once, so nobody anchors on the first opinion.

🎯 Consensus indicators

ScrumFight recognises full consensus, near-consensus, wide spreads and runs of ? or ☕ cards, so you know immediately whether to move on or talk.

👀 Spectator mode

Product Owners, Scrum Masters and stakeholders can watch without voting. The countdown does not wait for them.

🏷️ Named rounds

Give each round the story’s name or ticket number and find it back in the history.

😄 Emoji reactions

React to a reveal with context-aware emoji.

⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts

1–9 to pick a card, R to reveal, N for a new round.

🕐 Late joiners

People who join in the middle of a round are marked, so the result stays clear.

Insight

📊 Round statistics

Average, minimum, maximum, most chosen value and the distribution per card after every reveal.

📜 Round history

A scrollable list of every round in the session, useful in sprint planning and the retrospective.

📈 Personal statistics

With an account: rounds played, favourite card, consensus rate, activity heatmap and your “poker personality”.

🏅 Badges

19 badges to unlock, from your first round to the night owl.

Rooms and accounts

🚪 Join without an account

Enter a name and the room code and you are in. Works in any modern browser, on desktop and phone.

🔗 Share by code or link

Every room has its own six-character code and invite link.

💾 Saved rooms

With an account your team’s room stays available until you delete it. Guest rooms disappear after one hour of inactivity.

🔐 Sign in your way

Email and password, Google or GitHub, with optional two-factor authentication.

🌐 English and Dutch

The whole app is available in both languages.

Estimation methods

Choose a scale per room. ? means “I don’t know yet”, ☕ means “time for a break”.
