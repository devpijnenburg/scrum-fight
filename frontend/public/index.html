What is ScrumFight?

ScrumFight is a free, real-time Planning Poker tool for Scrum and Agile teams. Estimate user stories together during backlog refinement and sprint planning: everyone picks a card at the same time, cards are revealed only when the team is ready, and differences show up instantly. No installation and no account needed.

How it works

  1. 1. Create a room

    Pick a name and an estimation method: Fibonacci, Fibonacci+, T-shirt sizes or Powers of 2.

  2. 2. Share the code

    Send the room code or link to your team. Colleagues join from their browser, in the office or remotely.

  3. 3. Vote and reveal

    Everyone votes in secret. On reveal you instantly see whether there is consensus, and where the conversation needs to go.

Built for Scrum events

🔍 Backlog refinement

Estimate new stories quickly and without anchoring. Big differences between cards show where things are still unclear, so you know which questions to ask.

📋 Sprint planning

Estimate the last stories for the sprint and name every round, so you can see exactly what was estimated afterwards.

🔄 Retrospective

Look back at round history and team statistics and discuss how your team estimates and where you often disagree.

🌍 Remote and hybrid teams

Works in any browser, on laptop and phone. Everyone votes at the same time, wherever they are.
